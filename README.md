# Global AI Columbus

Columbus chapter of the [Global AI Community](https://globalai.community). We bring together builders, architects, and leaders working with AI, from first experiments to production agentic systems.

## Meetups

We meet in person at **Leading EDJE**, Dublin, OH.
Schedule and RSVP: [Global AI Columbus events](https://globalai.community/chapters/columbus)

## Get Involved

- **Discord:** [Join the conversation](https://discord.gg/uvsQFTadX) for questions, announcements, and collaboration between meetups
- **GitHub:** Session materials, demos, and community projects live here
- **Speak:** Have something to share? Let us know on [Discord](https://discord.gg/uvsQFTadX)

## Repositories

| Repo | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| [.github](https://github.com/globalai-columbus/.github) | Community health files, governance, and code of conduct |

## Contributing

- Fork the repo and submit a pull request; no direct pushes to `main`
- Use issues for bugs, ideas, and requests
- Never commit secrets, keys, or proprietary code

## Governance

This community is run by its members. Rules, roles, and decisions are drafted in the open:

- [Governance](https://github.com/globalai-columbus/.github/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md)
- [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/globalai-columbus/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

Propose changes by opening an issue or PR against those documents, or discuss them in the governance channel on Discord.
