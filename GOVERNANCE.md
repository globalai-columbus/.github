# Governance

This document describes how the Global AI Community - Columbus works together. It is intended to provide a clear, lightweight process that can evolve with the community.

## Participation

Anyone who participates constructively in the community is welcome. Community members can contribute by sharing ideas, participating in discussions, improving projects, or helping organize activities. Participation is subject to the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Roles

- **Contributors** take part in discussions and contribute to community projects or activities.
- **Maintainers** help guide projects, review contributions, make day-to-day decisions, and help uphold community standards.
- **Organizers** coordinate community activities and may also serve as maintainers.

These are functional roles, not a hierarchy of community value. One person may hold more than one role.

Maintainers may invite contributors to take on a role based on their sustained contributions, judgment, and commitment to the community. Maintainers are responsible for documenting relevant decisions and ensuring that access to community resources is handled carefully.

## Decision-making

We aim to make decisions openly, respectfully, and with input from people affected by them.

- Routine project and operational decisions may be made by the maintainers responsible for that area.
- Significant changes should be proposed in an appropriate GitHub discussion, issue, or pull request so community members have an opportunity to share feedback.
- Participants should work toward consensus. If consensus cannot be reached in a reasonable discussion, the responsible maintainers may make the decision and explain their reasoning.
- Decisions involving safety, privacy, or Code of Conduct reports may be handled privately by maintainers to protect the people involved.

## Changes to this document

Anyone may propose a change to this governance document by opening a pull request. Maintainers will review proposals using the decision-making process above.
