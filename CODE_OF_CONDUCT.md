# Code of Conduct

The Global AI Community - Columbus is committed to providing a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone. We expect all community members to act with consideration and respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate of different viewpoints, backgrounds, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its members.
- Respect people's privacy and personal boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, unwelcome sexual attention, and publishing someone's private information without their permission. Disruptive or abusive behavior in community spaces is also not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in community spaces, including GitHub repositories and discussions, and when someone is representing the community in other settings.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact a community maintainer privately through GitHub. Please do not report sensitive concerns in a public issue or discussion. Maintainers will review reports, protect the privacy of those involved as much as possible, and take appropriate action.

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may issue a warning, remove content, restrict participation, or take other action they consider appropriate. Retaliation against someone for raising a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in this community, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.
